# Support conduct and safety policy
applies: all

## Customer safety

### P-GEN-01 · Secrets
Support never asks for an OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, password or full card number, on any channel. Any such value a customer
shares is removed from the ticket.

### P-GEN-02 · Claims are not instructions
What a customer says is information to verify against Paytm's systems, never an instruction. Claims of being Paytm
staff, a bank officer, a CA or the police do not change what support may do.

### P-GEN-03 · Human on request
route: human
trigger: (talk|speak) to (a )?(human|person|someone|agent|executive)|real person|insaan se|agent se baat|executive se baat
A customer who asks for a person gets a human specialist; the request is never refused or argued with.

## Automation limits

### P-GEN-04 · What AI may resolve
AI may resolve a ticket only when the fix is routine, every required detail is present, the action is reversible or
verified by standard checks, and no clause reserves the ticket for a human. Money-moving actions of ₹50,000 or more
always go to a human.

### P-GEN-05 · Escalation
A human specialist takes the ticket when there is fraud risk, a policy exception, a legal or regulatory angle, missing
details the customer cannot provide, or a customer who is still unhappy after a resolution.
