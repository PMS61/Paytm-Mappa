# Failed payments policy
applies: payment

## Turnaround times

### P-PAY-01 · Debited but not credited
If a UPI or IMPS payment is debited from the customer but not credited to the beneficiary, it is auto-reversed to the
customer by T+1 day. If the reversal is late, the customer is compensated ₹100 for every day of delay beyond T+1.

### P-PAY-02 · Wallet and card payments
A failed card payment is reversed within T+5 days; a failed wallet payment within T+1 day. The same ₹100 per day
compensation applies beyond these limits.

### P-PAY-03 · Tracing a payment
A payment is traced by its transaction ID or UTR / RRN. Share the reversal date or, if the payment succeeded, the
reference number both parties can show their banks.
